❌ The Agony of Tab-Thrashing

• 35 open tabs: Competitor pages, Reddit reviews, SEC filings, and pricing matrices.

• Clipboard hell: Ctrl+C ➔ Alt+Tab ➔ Ctrl+V × 60 into a messy scratchpad.

• Context pollution: Pasting whole web pages into Claude/ChatGPT dumps cookie banners, headers, and navigation garbage.

• Lost provenance: 2 hours later, you can't find which specific competitor page had that vital stat.

Result: Exhausting mental friction, prompt hallucinations, and hours wasted formatting raw text.

✨ The Patens Flow State

• 1-Click capture: Highlight exact key paragraphs as you browse with Ctrl+Shift+Click. Auto-ingested with origin URLs attached.

• Forgiving Undo Window: 40-second grace window to inspect, pause, or prune clips without breaking flow.

• Hierarchical Command Palette: Hit Ctrl+Shift+Space to browse clustered documents or granular paragraphs.

• 1-Key AI Injection: Press Enter to stream structured, cited Markdown directly into Claude, ChatGPT, or your IDE.

Result: Deep, multi-source synthesis in minutes with 100% verified source traceability.
