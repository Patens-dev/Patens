Zero-Friction Capture

No more messy copy-pasting. Highlight text on StackOverflow, API docs, or GitHub issues, press Ctrl+Shift+C, and it instantly vectorizes into your local database.

Dynamic Workspace Mounting

Your web research automatically synchronizes to a local _context folder. Tell your AI to "Mount Patens context," and it dynamically targets your active project. Just tag the master 00_Context_Index.md map to give it total recall.

100% Local & Private

Powered by local SQLite-vec and FastEmbed. Your browsing history and API research never leave your machine until you explicitly query them with your AI assistant.

Patens is an open-source tool utilizing the Model Context Protocol (MCP).
